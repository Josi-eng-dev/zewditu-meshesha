ABOUT Zewditu Meshesha Children and Family Welfare and Development Association

Name of the organization

“Yezewditu Meshesha Yehitsanatna Beteseb Yebego Adragotna Limat Mahber” was registered by the Agency of Charities and societies with a registration number first 3023 in 2010 and later during registration period with reg. number 0528.
Since its establishment in 1991 Zewditu Meshesha Yehitsanatna Beteseb Bego Adragotna Limat Mahber (Zewditu Meshesha Children and Family Welfare Development Association) has fostered over 250 children. Most of these children are orphans and vulnerable coming from very poor families. Zewditu had fostered these children without any support from any organization except from her friends and relatives. She sold her house and her two automobiles for the same purpose…

OUR PROJECTS

Some of our projects.

Education

Education charities make learning possible for students of all ages, from pre-school to graduate school. They also provide other educational services and opportunities that help make schools more effective and more accessible to students of all backgrounds.

Home for Homeless

Homelessness is first and foremost a poverty issue that disproportionately affects children. Building upon the immediate need for providing basic shelter, Homes for the Homeless has enriched our services to provide wraparound support through community.
