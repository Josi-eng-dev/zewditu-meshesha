## Key Content Blocks

### 1. Organizational Identity & History
- **Full Legal Name:** Zewditu Meshesha Children and Family Welfare and Development Association
- **Amharic Name:** የዘውዲቱ መሸሻ የህፃናትና ቤተሰብ በጎ አድራጎትና ልማት ማህበር
- **Registration Authority:** Federal Agency for Civil Society Organizations (ACSO) / Agency of Charities and Societies
- **Registration Number:** Reg. No. 0528 (initially registered as 3023 in 2010)
- **Founding Date:** 1991 G.C.
- **Founder:** Emebereket Zewditu Meshesha
- **Total Children Supported to Date:** Over 250 orphans and vulnerable children directly fostered, with broader community support programs.

### 2. Strategic Objectives
- Fostering orphan and vulnerable children in a loving family-style environment.
- Providing comprehensive basic needs: nutrition, healthcare, education, counseling, and shelter.
- Empowering disadvantaged mothers and families through financial literacy, community meal-sharing, and vocational support.
- Facilitating community reintegration and self-reliance.

### 3. Program Areas
- **Daycare & Early Childhood Education:** Providing safe nursery care for children of low-income working mothers.
- **Educational Support:** School uniforms, stationery, tutoring, and school fee sponsorship.
- **Nutrition & Feeding:** Daily wholesome meals for children and emergency community grain/food sharing.
- **Healthcare & Psycho-social Support:** Collaboration with local medical centers (e.g. St. Peter's Hospital) for free checkups and psychological counseling.

### 4. Donations & Financial Information
- **Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE):** Verified account details on `/donate/`.
- **Telebirr & Local Payment Gateways:** Mobile money contribution numbers.
- **Pledge Form:** Downloadable form ("የተስፋ ሰነድ") for monthly sponsorships and recurring pledges.

### 5. News & Stories
- Total 30 published articles covering official partnerships (e.g., Ethio Telecom educational donations, Enat Bank financial literacy training, St. Peter's Hospital medical outreach, 85th birthday celebration of Founder Zewditu Meshesha).
